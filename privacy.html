Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

🔒 Our Fundamental Privacy Guarantee: DevFormat Pro processes 100% of your JSON, CSV, JWT, and Base64 data locally in your browser. We have no backend ingestion servers. Your code, proprietary data, and authentication tokens are never uploaded, logged, or visible to us or any third party.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Because all utility functions operate purely on the client side using JavaScript Web APIs, DevFormat Pro does not collect, transmit, store, or share:

  • Any JSON data or payloads pasted into the tool editors.
  • Any JWT tokens, signatures, or user claims.
  • Any CSV spreadsheets, database records, or Base64 strings.
  • Any personally identifiable information (PII) contained in your input text.

We use third-party advertising companies, specifically Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies and web beacons to collect non-personally identifiable information (such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, and pages visited) to serve targeted advertisements based on your prior visits to this and other websites across the internet.

Google's Use of the DART Cookie:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on DevFormat Pro.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Web Analytics

We may utilize privacy-friendly web analytics tools (such as Cloudflare Web Analytics or Google Analytics) to aggregate anonymous metrics such as page visits, bounce rates, and referring websites. This information is strictly utilized to optimize site performance and fix user interface bugs.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users possess rights regarding their personal data. Because we do not store or process personal data on servers, we do not sell or monetize personal information. If you have questions regarding your rights, you may contact our data privacy officer at privacy@devformatpro.com.

Our site may contain links to external sites (such as GitHub, documentation guides, or advertiser links). If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us, and we strongly advise you to review their respective Privacy Policies.
